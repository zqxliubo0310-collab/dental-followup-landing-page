Common questions

Unscheduled treatment follow-up, explained.

What is dental patient follow-up?

Dental patient follow-up is the process of contacting patients after an inquiry or visit to agree on the next step. It can include returning missed calls, checking whether a patient wants to schedule treatment already discussed with the practice, and contacting patients overdue for recall. Each opportunity needs an owner, a next action and a recorded outcome.

What is unscheduled treatment follow-up?

Unscheduled treatment follow-up is the process of working diagnosed treatment plans that were presented but never scheduled, giving each case an owner, a next action and a recorded outcome, so accepted treatment does not sit untouched.

How do I find unscheduled treatment in Dentrix?

Pull the Dentrix unscheduled treatment report, filter for presented treatment with no appointment, and work the list in priority order by expiry and value. An appointment for other work does not close the plan on that report. Sort the first batch by value, expiry, insurance remaining, and touch history, then give it an owner and an outcome on a working batch.

How do I pull the Open Dental unscheduled treatment report?

Use the Open Dental Treatment Finder for planned procedures that have not been scheduled, filter by insurance remaining and procedure code, and give the resulting list a named owner and a recorded outcome for each case.

How do I run an unscheduled treatment report in Open Dental?

See our step-by-step guide to running an unscheduled treatment report in Open Dental. Once you have the report, the next step is assigning follow-up owners and tracking appointment outcomes.

How is follow-up recovery different from appointment reminders?

Appointment reminders concern visits that are already booked. Follow-up recovery addresses unresolved opportunities, such as an unanswered inquiry, treatment that has not been scheduled or an overdue patient who has not returned. See our dental recall and recovery comparison.

Where should a dental practice start?

Start with unscheduled treatment or missed calls. Overdue recall can wait. Use the dental revenue leak calculator for an aggregate estimate, or request a free workflow review. Neither needs patient-level data. A patient-level queue is a proposed pilot and is confirmed before any export.
